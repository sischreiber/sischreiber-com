pmcli - Product Management From the Command Line#

Late July 2026

Product managers spend their days moving between a growing number of SaaS tools, each with its own interface. pmcli brings the everyday work into a single terminal instead.

A set of commands covers recurring tasks such as clustering app store reviews, tracking OKR progress, writing a user story or building a changelog. The AI runs quietly in the background rather than through a chat window. The idea is simple: product work does not always need another dashboard.

Clustering Product Opportunities With Embeddings#

Product ideas often describe the same underlying problem in different words, spread across the backlog with no obvious connection. Why not group them with embeddings, to catch duplicates and see where customer problems actually cluster?

Each new issue becomes a vector. Ideas about the same customer problem sit close together, a semantic similarity that holds even when the descriptions share no words. The clusters show which problems come up most often and where the real gaps are.
